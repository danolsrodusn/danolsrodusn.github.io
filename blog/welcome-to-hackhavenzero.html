Welcome to
HackHavenZero.

A place for the lessons you only learn by doing.

For years I've been building things: apps, teams, a company. I've rarely written about them. This blog is where that changes.

HackHavenZero is a place for the lessons you only learn by doing. Organising student hackathons taught me some. Co-founding Rada Labs taught me more, and so did the pivots we made along the way. Now I'm working as a Principal Consultant, and I'm still learning.

What you can expect

  • Founder notes: what building a startup is really like, including the parts that didn't work.
  • Consulting from the inside: how a founder mindset helps, and sometimes gets in the way, when you work with clients.
  • Tech & innovation: hackathons, prototypes, and turning ideas into products people use.

Posts will be short and practical. If something here is useful to you, or you disagree, reach out on LinkedIn.
